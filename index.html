Flagship Project — IQ-PARC

Modernizing Quantum Education for Middle School

NSF Award #2422937  •  Oct 2024 – Present  •  Purdue University

The Challenge

Quantum mechanics underlies modern technology from lasers to semiconductors — but it has essentially never been taught below the college level. Middle schoolers had no on-ramp into foundational quantum concepts.

The Product

A complete 3-week curriculum — lesson plans plus paired pre/post assessments — for 6th and 8th grade. Now entering its 3rd and final version this semester ahead of classroom rollout.

Curriculum development with subject-matter experts

Quantum Physics Professor

validates scientific accuracy of every concept

STEM Education Professor

validates pedagogical design and grade-level fit

Kim — Instructional Designer

translates between the two and builds the curriculum

Making the invisible understandable

Every abstract concept is anchored to something students physically did in class — then assessed against that same anchor, so the question tests understanding, not recall.

PhET Double-Slit Simulation

Teaches
Wave-particle duality

Students fire single photons at two slits and watch the interference pattern emerge over time — direct evidence that light behaves as both particle and wave.

UV Bead Demonstration

Teaches
Photon energy & wavelength

Beads change color under UV light but not visible light of the same intensity — concrete evidence that higher-energy photons drive the reaction.

NextGen Spectra Gas Tubes

Teaches
Atomic emission spectra

Hydrogen and helium gas tubes glow different colors, showing each element has a unique set of electron energy levels.

AIDesign

Generative AI for Art + STEM Education

Self-initiated research collaboration • Nov 2022 – Aug 2023

How can AI image-generation tools support creativity in K-12 art education — without defaulting to text-based LLMs, which dominate most classroom AI conversations?

  • Pioneered instructional strategies built on image synthesis (Stable Diffusion), not text generation
  • Embedded prompt engineering directly into the curriculum for 5th-grade learners
  • Piloted with a student cohort and delivered professional development for one teacher
  • Used learning analytics on students' prompt data to refine instructional strategies

Published: "Prompt Aloud! Incorporating image-generative AI into STEAM class with learning analytics using prompt data" — Education and Information Technologies (2023)

AIData

Educational Chatbot for STEM Problem-Solving

Master's thesis • Funded by KETI • 2022 – 2023

How do a chatbot's humanness and question type shape learning outcomes and motivation, using the ICAP framework (passive, active, constructive, interactive)?

  • Designed a 2×2 experimental study (N=60) on AI humanness and cognitive load
  • Designed the dialogue logic and built a rule-based chatbot to scaffold STEM problem-solving
  • Ran mixed-methods analysis: performance metrics + qualitative user interviews
  • Finding: constructive learning outperformed active learning; humanness didn't always help

Published: "The Effects of Cognitive Engagement and Humanness of Chatbots on Learning Outcomes and Motivation" — IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies (2025)

AIData

CourseMIRROR — NLP-Scaffolded Reflection

NSF Award Abstract #2329274 • Oct 2023 – Oct 2024

Does mobile-assisted, structured self-reflection improve undergraduate academic performance in large lecture courses?

  • Designed a quasi-experimental study using a pre/post-test methodology to isolate learning gains
  • Applied LIWC (Linguistic Inquiry & Word Count) analysis to quantify cognitive patterns in student text
  • Led statistical analysis (ANOVA, regression) across Physics and Psychology cohorts
  • Evaluated engagement via submission frequency and specificity/depth of reflections

Co-authored: "Enhancing Student Reflections with Natural Language Processing based Scaffolding" — Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence (2025)
